## Current research

### Explainable multimodal gait analysis

My principal MSc research investigates EEG, EMG, IMU and force-plate representations for remote gait assessment. The study uses deterministic subject-wise folds across unimodal and multimodal experiments, evaluates conventional machine-learning and 1D-CNN approaches, and applies SHAP to random-forest models.

### Privacy-preserving IoT security

I am also developing a closed-loop research framework connecting explainable threat detection, data governance, human-in-the-loop decision support and guardrailed LLM-assisted remediation with verification, rollback and auditable escalation.
